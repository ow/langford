One regular-workflow gap is independent of the diarizer choice. The installed Parakeet-MLX0.5.1 CLI already exports token text, start, end, duration and confidence inside each sentence. Its sentence splitter also supports `--max-duration`, `--max-words` and `--silence-gap`. The pipeline's `LocalDiarizer._run_transcription` currently reduces that output to sentence start/end/text, and its temporary original CLI output is then deleted. Later recovery cannot safely recover word boundaries from the saved coarse sentence alone.

Preserve the original token evidence and actual STT version/model provenance before changing speaker assignments. A following stage can align measured tokens against validated speaker turns, retain genuinely mixed/overlapping words as unresolved, and assemble supported utterances without assigning a whole multi-person paragraph to its dominant voice. Shorter CLI sentence limits can reduce pathological blocks, but a fixed duration does not establish a speaker boundary. This is an additive future-workflow change; historical rows require explicit source-bound reconciliation, not destructive replacement.

Keep acoustic timing support and personal identity admission separate. A better diarizer can improve boundaries while still attaching an incorrect name if its enrollment bank is wrong. The frozen direct-only verifier admits only bounded, source-backed introductions and continues to hold uncalibrated cross-recording propagation. Expand independently admitted enrollment across distinct recordings, then measure held-out same-person and different-person cases before permitting propagation. A small pair of identities cannot establish an archive-wide false-positive rate.

Fine timing units need not become individual search embeddings. Keep private token provenance, public speaker utterances, and semantic retrieval chunks as separate representations so improved attribution does not multiply retrieval/index costs unnecessarily.

No shared transcription/ingester/worker code was changed for this finding. The benchmark completes the original three-engine comparison first; these pipeline improvements should have their own meaningful owner-boundary regressions and source-preserving rollout.
