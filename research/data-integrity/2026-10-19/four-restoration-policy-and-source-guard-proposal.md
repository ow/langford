## Ordering and final capture

The actor-only five-case lane goes first, including independent whole after-readback. Only afterward may the restoration owner recapture the four targets, full affected-meeting witness rows, all full votes/IDs, source/URL/runtime/store hashes and actual generated-expression/index/trigger contract. Any change requires new explicit review. No final before is frozen by this design.

The current FTS expression must be read from the actual catalog at that later capture, then bound again inside any approved transaction. Do not reuse old1505 expression assumptions. Expected text_search must be derived from the **entire accepted new text** and unchanged summary/other inputs under that captured expression, for example through a separately reviewed temporary generated-column rehearsal. Generated-column writes are not proposed. The expected generated delta, expression and relevant function/index/trigger definitions must all be reviewed before a live update.

## Exact allowed restoration policy

Keep IDs, meeting/agenda links, mover/seconder, result/disposition, summaries, existing audit metadata and every full vote row/ID unchanged. Propose only the complete accepted text, minimal raw canonical identity/restoration evidence, active embedding NULL after an approved text change, and database-managed target updated_at. Preserve exact old text/meta/vector in immutable private full-before and restoration-history receipts; an old embedding is archived, never discarded or silently kept active for different text. No replacement embedding call is authorized.

The future metadata schema is:

- `canonical_source_motion`: the existing recomputable **raw** source identity, retaining original PDF hash, raw body/block, raw offset/order, outcome and current key. Any conflicting existing identity holds; no previous_keys invention or silent overwrite.
- `canonical_text_restoration`: reviewed complete-native-transcription kind, source key/PDF hash, raw-body hash, accepted-full-body hash, root identity/body review hashes, approved full-transcription receipt hash and final full-before/history receipt hash.
- `canonical_text_restoration_history`: append the exact prior full text and prior source/restoration pointers, with hash-bound complete metadata/vector before evidence; retain every existing history entry and unrelated branch. An unexpected metadata shape holds rather than being coerced.

Do not add or change individual_vote_coverage, ballot_reconstruction, vote_record coverage or votes. Accepted identity/body evidence does not certify a ballot.

## Separately reviewed generic transcription adapter design

The proposed adapter would validate an approved source-bound full-transcription receipt only **after** the ordinary raw canonical occurrence identity and its own formal envelope are re-proved. It supplies documentary transcription evidence, not new legacy-row identity authority.

Receipt keys and proof must include original PDF SHA/format, cleaned-text hash, exact raw block/body hashes, current raw offset/order/key, whole original envelope hash, complete accepted output hash, original native page/word geometry, exact nonoverlapping consumed raw→accepted spans, source-pixel page/clip/render-recipe hashes and independent/root source-review receipt hashes. It must replay those exact spans into the full raw body and verify the entire stored body equals the approved output, with every other character unchanged. Unknown, missing, tampered or stale receipt, source, span, geometry or render evidence means hold. No new OCR or model call is needed or authorized.

Approved receipts would live in a reviewed repository store discovered by original source hash and exact original occurrence fields. Metadata cannot approve its own receipt. Adapter bytes and the complete sorted receipt set/content hashes must join the ordinary runtime/cache guard, so additions, deletions and modifications invalidate old cached candidates or holds. Public runtime evidence must not depend on private paths.

One shared predicate could then support **bound** stored-body equality in bound_source_motion and the canonical existing-row source_key_body_conflict check. Canonical planning must preserve the reviewed corrected full body on subsequent reingestion. Do not add corrected text to generic unbound source_motion_candidates, mutate source['text']/blocks/operative offsets, recover skipped introductions, create a motion or change a key. No motion-ID whitelist, fuzzy/compact/containment alias or broad223 admission is proposed.

The exact transcription-validation receipt hash must propagate separately into source proof. Original formal/body/outcome ownership, source date/organization, roster identity and dated membership, physical attendance/session chronology, policy and result/tally guards remain active. A hypothetical accepted transcription with a wrong roster/date/arrival state still holds. 3457/3459 duplicate ownership, competing legacy claims and all unrelated body holds remain unaffected.

This is a schema and integration proposal only. Adapter implementation, its source-bound regressions, restoration applier/restore code, final plans, any mutation rehearsal and any live update each need the separate review requested by root. No execution is supplied here.
