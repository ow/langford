## Two independent decisions

**Record identity:** does one existing legacy row describe one uniquely owned original formal occurrence? This decision does not certify the legacy summary, approve a native transcription, or authorize changing a row.

**Restorable body:** is the entire operative resolution, including every condition, number, negation and incorporated subclause, independently proved and transcribed cleanly enough to become canonical text? A positive identity decision cannot clear a body hold. Raw native text can contain extraction defects even when its original formal occurrence is certain.

Text containment, compact equality, shared wording, a unique actor pair, the first or last text match, and a legacy agenda title/number are never identity authority. They may locate a review lead. Legacy AI-derived metadata supplies a claim to test against original evidence, not proof that the claim is correct.

## Joint native identity requirements

All requirements must hold together for an identity review to pass:

1. **Original artifact and coordinate systems.** Recheck exact original PDF bytes, URL sidecar and source date/organization. Retain original native page/word geometry and exact source quotes. Keep original geometry offsets and ordinary cleaned-text offsets separately labeled; never apply one coordinate system to another. The raw PDF, extracted text, formal blocks and operative offsets remain unchanged.
2. **Whole governing item.** Prove the entire printed heading, including wrapped title/address/filing lines, its parent hierarchy and preceding/following item boundaries from original native geometry. A numbered condition inside a resolution is not an agenda heading. The candidate occurrence must lie inside the original governing item's actual bounds. A current agenda_item_id or AI-derived title cannot substitute for this proof.
3. **Typed identifier and action.** An original filing identifier, bylaw number/version or other reviewed native identifier must agree with the existing row's explicit claim. Match the full identifier and its role: a background Zoning Bylaw No. 300 citation is not the subject application. Adoption, first reading, preparing a bylaw, giving notice and issuing a permit remain different actions. Companion bylaws, addresses, amendment numbers and action stages must be accounted for without inventing equivalence.
4. **Owned original formal occurrence.** Bind the exact mover/seconder or separately supported formal frame, entire body and its own compatible outcome to one original envelope. Preserve source SHA, raw offsets, source_order, current key and full quote. Prove the envelope independently of whether the parser recognized every mover prefix. A second matching envelope remains a competitor until its distinct occurrence ownership is established.
5. **Actors and outcome.** Resolve original actor quotes to dated same-organization canonical people without fuzzy spelling or inferred attendance. Test the legacy actor claims against these identities. Require the occurrence's own compatible outcome; an adjacent procedural vote cannot provide it. Neither actors nor outcome alone identifies the row.
6. **Full meeting uniqueness.** Inventory every original formal envelope, including unparsed leads, and every existing legacy row in the meeting, including complete rows, unresolved rows, repeated applications and alternative motions. Enumerate all source-to-row claims in both directions. Exactly one existing row may own this occurrence under the complete reviewed identifier/action/item/actor contract, and that row may have exactly one qualifying original occurrence. Missing inventory, a competing claim or an unexplained existing source key means hold. Do not count only the seven selected rows or the remaining-ballot cohort.
7. **Native hierarchy, formal introductions and record adjacency.** Retain the immediate original preceding/following governing items and formal introductions, including introductions whose main motion is still pending, as well as the next completed formal envelope. These are distinct witnesses. Represent the pending-main, amendment, amendment outcome and main-motion resumption/outcome relationships explicitly; never skip an unclosed original introduction to reach the next completed envelope or attach its amendment outcome to the pending main motion. Preserve every intervening original item, even if a prior heading inventory omitted it. Corroborate the legacy row's position using independently source-bound neighboring records where available. Database ID order, timestamps and AI agenda ordering alone are insufficient. Missing, skipped or contradictory hierarchy/adjacency remains a review hold; do not force a monotonic sequence to resolve a collision.

Passing these requirements establishes a reviewed correspondence only. It must not become a generic text fallback or a broad alias rule. Any later restoration needs its own explicit plan and approval.
