# Langford recording conventions: verified scope

Research checked the City’s current bylaw directory, official procedure bylaw,
2021 agenda source documents, and targeted official-source searches. This is an
evidence boundary for machine recovery, not permission to equate meeting-level
attendance with presence for a particular vote.

## Bylaw1971

The current directory still links the May9,2024 consolidation. It was adopted
October18,2021, repealing819; its register lists2051 (March21,2022),2138
(August21,2023),2175 (May9,2024). Section44 requires dissenters to be identified;
60(i–j) require present members to vote except conflicts and treat failure to
indicate a vote as support. Original2021 section38 already required dissent
names; this was renumbered later. These rules support a conditional inference
only when the full eligible roster at that precise item, departures/returns,
recusals and complete source block are established.

- [Current City directory](https://langford.ca/city-hall/bylaws-directory/).
- [Official consolidated1971](https://langford.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Procedure-Bylaw-No.-1971-Consolidated-to-20240509.pdf), cover, printed pages11,13,16.
- [Official October18,2021 agenda](https://www.langford.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/20211018-Special-Agenda-Package-2.pdf), package page164 section38. Locally cached in `archive/langford/Council/2021/10/2021-10-18 530pm Special Council [3e61e99f]/agenda.md`.

## Bylaw819 and later amendments

No complete primary-source819 text was located publicly in this pass. Official
2016 amendment1654 changes meeting times, and2020 amendment1701 changes
pandemic/electronic participation. Neither establishes a dissent-recording
requirement. Do not extrapolate1971 to pre-October18,2021 meetings.

- [Official2016 amendment1654](https://langford.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/20160815-Regular-Council-Agenda-Package.pdf), page116.
- [Official2020 amendment1701](https://www.langford.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/20200316-Council-Procedure-amendment.pdf).

No post-May2024 procedure amendment appeared in the targeted official searches
or available2025–2026 Council agenda/minutes cache. The current directory still
publishes the2024 consolidation. This absence does **not** prove there were no
later amendments. The verified register boundary remains May9,2024; retain this
uncertainty in provenance for subsequent meetings and seek an authoritative
updated register before asserting a complete effective-period history.
