# Historical Langford voting rules: evidence boundary

The full official text of Procedure Bylaw819(2004) has not been located. Pre-October18,2021 full-ballot inference from a carried result and absent dissent names remains unsupported. No database or production policy changes were made by this research.

## What primary sources establish

The [October4,2021 staff report](https://www.langford.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/20211004-Agenda-Package-Open-530-pm.pdf), package pp294–296, describes the2004 procedure bylaw and minor amendments passed in2016 and2020. The attached replacement is1971; it does not reproduce819. Its later mandatory dissent-naming clause must not be applied backwards.

[Amendment1654](https://langford.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/20160815-Regular-Council-Agenda-Package.pdf), p116, replaces the meeting schedule/start-time paragraph. [September6,2016 minutes reproduced in the September19 agenda](https://langford.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/20160919-Agenda-Package-Council-Redacted.pdf), package p17, explicitly record its adoption. This amendment does not establish voting or dissent-recording rules.

[Amendment1701](https://www.langford.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/20200316-Council-Procedure-amendment.pdf), final page, adds pandemic/local-emergency electronic participation. The signed March26,2020 minutes explicitly record adoption; the original PDF and official URL are saved in the evidence ledger. This amendment also does not establish dissent-recording completeness.

The [Community Charter's current section123](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/03026_05#section123) requires present members to vote, treats nonindication as affirmative, and applies its voting rules to council committees. The [official amendment history](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/03026_pit) traces the section to January1,2004 and reproduces the earlier subsection4 wording before a2023 pronoun amendment. This provides a statutory present-voting basis independent of1971. It does not require complete individual names in minutes, establish a committee's appointments, or prove item-level attendance.

Native PDF/HTML source paths, official URLs, binary hashes, page bounds and bounded quotes are recorded in `historical-819-primary-amendment-evidence.json` and `historical-voting-statute-evidence.json`.

## Contradictory discovery lead, not a primary rule

A [contemporaneous May10,2008 article by Steven Hurdle](https://insidelangford.wordpress.com/2008/05/10/things-not-recorded/) says the author obtained819 from City Hall. It reproduces voting subsections treating abstentions as affirmative but recording individual ayes/nays only on request. It also reports a Seaton dissent missing from the published minutes. No original scan or official819 link accompanies it.

This is secondary evidence and cannot certify819's text, scope or effective version. It is a concrete warning against assuming that silence in old minutes means unanimity. The article content hash and narrow reproduction lead are preserved in `historical-819-rule-boundary.json`.

## Discovery limits

Current official bylaw/media searches expose1971 and the two amendments, not819. Searches of cached agenda appendices found the replacement1971, not a complete819 copy. Browser access to the historic City-domain archive index returned seven unrelated819-number URLs and an empty procedure-name result. Shell archive requests timed out; Common Crawl returned503/504 errors. These failures and narrow results do not prove no archived copy exists under another filename.

## How to close the remaining gap

Obtain from Langford Legislative Services the complete adopted819 text, its adoption date, its amendment/consolidation register through October18,2021, and certified adopted1654/1701 copies. Request clarification of the contemporaneous minutes/recorded-vote provisions and Council/Committee-of-the-Whole application. Historical committee eligibility still requires the relevant appointment records or terms of reference. No request has been sent.

Until those primary records are available, recover explicit named individual choices and separately source-verified unanimous ballots with a proven eligible per-vote roster. Keep other historical choices unknown; a carried outcome remains a real decision even when its individual roll call was never recorded.
