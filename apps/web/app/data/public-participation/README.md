# Published public participation inventories

These curated records link pre-service resident appearances to a specific person
and municipality. They are separate from council votes, attendance and speaking
statistics. Rendering and filtering use no AI calls or additional database reads.

## Lisa Foxall

Person 353, Langford. The 54 meetings come from
`research/lisa-foxall-speaking.csv` and the accompanying analysis notes. Every
meeting date, municipality and minutes URL was checked against the database's
official minutes on 2026-10-18; each source names L. Foxall or Lisa Foxall. Existing
research also supplies self-identification and cross-meeting voice evidence for
this identity. Chris Foxall (person 41) is a different person and is excluded.

The public summaries paraphrase the minutes, removing addresses, clipped endings,
subsequent speakers and procedural text that leaked into the research excerpts.
They are not direct quotations. Topics suggested by machine transcripts are not
used to expand these statements beyond the minutes evidence.

The research's 131 timed blocks and 153.7-minute estimate remain provisional and
are deliberately not published as verified speech or included in profile totals.
Audio review is required before publishing exact quotations or timed attribution.

To extend this inventory, check a named public-participation entry in the official
minutes, confirm the person's identity, and add the meeting date, summary and both
source links. Keep the scope/coverage note current. A full name or initial match
alone does not establish identity; do not automatically import candidate matches.
