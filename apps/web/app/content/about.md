Council agendas, minutes and video are public, but they're hard to follow as a stack of PDFs. Open Langford is an independent project by Owen that makes Langford's council meetings searchable and easier to follow. It's not an official City of Langford service, and the City's own records are always the final authority.

Open Langford is a fork of [ViewRoyal.ai](https://github.com/kpeatt/viewroyal), the open-source project Kyle built to make View Royal's council easier to follow. It runs separately, with its own Langford data and changes. Thanks to Kyle for building it and sharing the code.
